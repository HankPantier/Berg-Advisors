## LinkedIn

If you're spending $40,000 a month on Amazon PPC and Meta ads, a 2% cash back card puts $800 back into your effective ad cost every single month. That's not free money to ignore until January, it's a line item that belongs on your books, coded correctly against the expense it offset.

Most multi-channel sellers we work with treat rewards as an afterthought. The better move: match your card's bonus categories to where your money actually goes (ad spend, freight, overseas supplier payments), then reconcile those rewards monthly the same way you reconcile Amazon and Shopify payouts. Skip that step and cash back can end up misclassified as income, which inflates your top line right when a bank or investor is looking at your financials.
