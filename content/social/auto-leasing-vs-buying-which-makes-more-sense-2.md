## LinkedIn

A $45,000 cargo van bought with 80% business use can qualify for a full first-year deduction under Section 179. Lease that same van, and you only deduct the business-use share of each payment, spread evenly over the term. For sellers running Amazon, Shopify, and Walmart storefronts, that gap shows up fast once Q4 margin is gone and a Q1 supplier invoice lands.

Andrew Berg, CPA, breaks down when buying beats leasing for e-commerce owners hauling inventory to a 3PL, and when a lease's simpler math actually wins. Vehicle weight matters here too: cargo vans over 6,000 pounds GVWR often qualify for a bigger deduction than a passenger SUV doing the same job.
