## LinkedIn

$1,500 to $4,000 a month. That's what we typically find in overlapping software subscriptions when we open the books for a multi-channel seller doing $2 million a year across Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart. A third of that spend is usually redundant: two inventory tools doing the same job because a bookkeeper set up one and an operations hire set up the other, and nobody compared notes.

This isn't a rounding error. It's margin you'd flag immediately if it showed up as a supplier price increase or a jump in Amazon referral fees.

Our fix: build a quarterly subscription audit into your regular bookkeeping close, so every recurring charge gets reviewed for usage, redundancy, and cheaper alternatives before it quietly renews again.
