## LinkedIn

One holding hitting 40 to 50 percent of an account after a strong run isn't a growth story, it's concentration risk. That's the pattern Berg Advisors sees constantly with multi-channel sellers: a Q4 payout goes into whatever fund performed well last year, then nobody touches it for three years. If 60 percent of your net worth is already tied up in your e-commerce brand, your outside portfolio probably needs to lean toward stability, not more correlated risk. Andrew Berg, CPA, walks through why rebalancing deserves the same attention as your ad spend and margins, plus a practical review cadence tied to your tax planning calendar.
