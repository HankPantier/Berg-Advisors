## LinkedIn

A private nursing home room now runs $10,000 to $12,000 a month in the Philadelphia suburbs. Medicare covers none of it past a short rehab stay.

That's a manageable number for a W-2 household with a diversified 401(k). It's a different problem for a multi-channel seller whose net worth sits in Amazon and Shopify inventory, a warehouse lease, or a 3PL contract. An unplanned $150,000 to $300,000 care event can mean pulling cash from the business at the worst possible time, right before a Q4 inventory buy, or selling product at a discount just to generate liquidity.

Long-term care insurance, self-funding, and the VA Aid and Attendance benefit each solve a piece of this, and most families combine two or three. Andrew C. Berg, CPA, walks through how to structure this without touching business working capital.
