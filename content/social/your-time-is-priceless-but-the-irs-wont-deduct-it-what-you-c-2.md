## LinkedIn

Your volunteer hours on a nonprofit board? Not deductible, no matter your hourly rate. That catches a lot of e-commerce owners off guard when they sponsor a 5K, sit on a board, or drive donated inventory to a shelter and assume none of it belongs on a tax return.

Here's what does count: mileage at 14 cents per mile, supplies bought for a fundraiser, required travel, and the cost basis of donated inventory. A board member driving roughly 16 miles each way to a quarterly meeting can rack up nearly 100 miles a month in service miles alone, often $150 to $300 a year sitting unclaimed.

Andrew C. Berg, CPA, breaks down what the IRS actually wants documented, including when a $250 acknowledgment letter or a $5,000 appraisal kicks in for donated inventory.
