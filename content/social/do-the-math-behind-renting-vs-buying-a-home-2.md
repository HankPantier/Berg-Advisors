## LinkedIn

A $650,000 home near Newtown Square runs roughly $4,600 a month in PITI. The equivalent rental costs $2,400. That gap looks simple until you factor in seasonal revenue.

For Amazon and Shopify sellers, the real test isn't your average month, it's your worst three consecutive months over the last two years. Mortgage underwriters average your tax returns; your bank account doesn't care about averages when February hits and November's cash is gone.

There's also the $130,000 down payment to weigh. If your inventory typically returns 15-20% through margin and turns, that money has a real opportunity cost sitting in a house instead of your business.

Run the numbers before you run the emotions. Full breakdown here: https://www.bergpartners.com/resources/do-the-math-behind-renting-vs-buying-a-home-2
