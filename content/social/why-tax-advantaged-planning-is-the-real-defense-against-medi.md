## LinkedIn

A $6,000 ER bill doesn't check your Q4 inventory schedule first.

For multi-channel sellers with lumpy revenue, that collision is real: a health crisis lands the same month as a Prime Day restock, and cash meant for product covers a hospital bill instead. An emergency fund only buys time. It doesn't lower what you actually owe.

HSAs, FSAs, and the itemized medical deduction do. For 2025, HSA contribution limits sit at $4,300 for self-only coverage and $8,550 for family coverage, growing tax-free and coming out tax-free for qualified expenses. The itemized deduction kicks in once unreimbursed costs clear 7.5% of AGI. On $200,000 in AGI, that's the first $15,000 that isn't deductible, everything past it is.

Andrew Berg, CPA, has seen where sellers get burned: not bad insurance, but never modeling what a medical event does to cash flow during restock season.
