## LinkedIn

Berg builds quarterly advisory calls into every engagement so someone with fresh eyes catches the flat revenue and creeping expenses before an owner even names the problem out loud. Fixed monthly fees mean a half-formed worry about cash flow costs nothing extra to raise. If you're running a multi-channel business on four platforms and haven't looked at your P&L with clear eyes in six weeks, that's worth a direct conversation.
