## LinkedIn

A seller doing $2.5M across Amazon, Shopify, and Walmart FBA called us convinced he was underinsured. Turns out he was paying for a storefront rider he didn't need and an umbrella policy sized for a net worth he'd left behind two years ago, while carrying zero coverage on $180,000 in FBA inventory. That's the pattern we see most with multi-channel sellers: insurance bought one policy at a time, after a scare, instead of against real exposure.

The fix isn't a longer checklist. It's sizing coverage to your net worth, inventory cost, and the losses that would force you into debt within 90 days. Amazon's own FBA reimbursement terms cap payouts well below replacement cost, so that gap needs a separate look.

We run this as part of quarterly advisory calls with e-commerce clients, because inventory value can swing 30% between Q1 and Q4. Read the full breakdown: https://www.bergpartners.com/resources/insuring-where-it-counts-avoid-over-insuring-in-the-wrong-pl-2
