## LinkedIn

A client in Bristol, PA once asked, half-joking, if the German Shepherd guarding his fulfillment warehouse counted as a business expense. Turns out the answer is real: a guard dog living at a separate warehouse, with training records and a documented security plan, can qualify as a legitimate deduction under IRS Publication 535. A family pet that visits the office on Tuesdays does not. Andrew Berg, CPA, notes that an undocumented deduction on something small, like dog food, can invite a closer look at your COGS methodology or multi-state sales tax nexus positions. For multi-channel e-commerce sellers storing inventory in real buildings, that's a bigger risk than the deduction is worth. Our latest post walks through guard dogs, service animals, and breeding operations, plus the paperwork the IRS actually wants to see before you claim any of it.
