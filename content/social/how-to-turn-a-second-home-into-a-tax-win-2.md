## LinkedIn

14 days. That's the line the IRS uses to decide if your Poconos or Jersey Shore place is a tax-free getaway or a rental property with a whole new set of rules. Rent it out for 14 days or fewer in a year and that income is completely tax-free, no Schedule E required. Cross that threshold and you're into expense limits, depreciation rules, and Schedule E reporting.

We see this constantly with e-commerce clients who take a strong Q4 and put it toward a second home. Andrew Berg, CPA, puts it plainly: the owners who get the most value are the ones who map out their usage calendar before closing, not after tax season starts. Fifteen days of personal use instead of fourteen changes everything.

If you're weighing a second property against your business income, read the full breakdown: https://www.bergpartners.com/resources/how-to-turn-a-second-home-into-a-tax-win-2
