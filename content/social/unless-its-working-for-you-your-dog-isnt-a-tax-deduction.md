## LinkedIn

A client's German Shepherd guards her overflow inventory in a Newtown Square warehouse. Her golden retriever at home does not qualify for a deduction, and that difference is exactly where the IRS draws the line under Publication 535.

We see this constantly with multi-channel sellers on Amazon, Shopify, and Walmart who lease storage outside the home and want to know what actually counts. A guard dog stationed at your fulfillment space can be a legitimate business expense, covering food, vet care, and training. A family pet that shows up in a product photo once a month cannot.

Andrew Berg, CPA, breaks down guard dogs, service animals, breeding operations, and brand mascots, plus the documentation that separates a real deduction from an audit flag.
