## LinkedIn

Rent your lake house for 14 days or fewer and the IRS doesn't tax a dime of it, even if you pocket $6,000 during a trade show week nearby. Cross 15 days, though, and personal-use days start deciding how much of your rental losses can actually offset your Amazon or Shopify profit. Most second-home guidance online assumes steady W-2 income. E-commerce sellers deal with revenue that spikes 40% in Q4 and drops off in February, so the math on a $650,000 property in the Poconos looks nothing like it does for a typical buyer. The scenario we see most often: 60 personal days and 20 rental days, which caps deductible losses at the rental income itself, not your business income. Get the classification right before closing, not after a summer of casual bookings. Andrew Berg, CPA, walks growing sellers through this exact calculation regularly.
