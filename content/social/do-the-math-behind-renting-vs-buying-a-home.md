## LinkedIn

Q4 revenue is in and the down payment looks tempting. Before you tour that Newtown Square colonial, run the numbers first. On a $650,000 home versus a $3,200/month rental, buying costs about $1,870 more per month once you add property tax, insurance, and maintenance reserves, before the $130,000 down payment even enters the picture. That capital sitting in a brokerage account at 7% is worth roughly $758 a month in opportunity cost. For multi-channel sellers, there's a second wrinkle: lenders average two years of net income from your tax returns, so a strong current year won't fully offset a slower prior one. Clean, accrual-adjusted books change how fast an underwriter trusts your numbers. Andrew Berg, CPA, walks through the full model, including the mortgage interest deduction math most calculators skip, in our latest post.
