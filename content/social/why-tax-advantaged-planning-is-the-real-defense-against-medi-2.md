## LinkedIn

A $14,000 surgery bill. $8,550 of it already sitting in a pre-tax HSA. That's what happened to one of our seven-figure Shopify and Amazon clients, an S-corp seller with payroll structured to run HSA contributions before the tax hit landed.

An emergency fund got them through the two-week cash gap. The tax structure covered the rest, because the remaining balance, combined with mileage and related costs, cleared the 7.5% AGI threshold and became a deductible medical expense.

For self-employed e-commerce owners on marketplace health plans, exposure to a medical crisis is open-ended. HSAs, FSAs, and the itemized medical deduction are what actually reduce what it costs, not just help you survive the month.
