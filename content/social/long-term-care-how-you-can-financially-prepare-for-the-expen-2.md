## LinkedIn

A private room in a Pennsylvania nursing home runs $130,000 to $145,000 a year in the Philadelphia suburbs. Assisted living averages $60,000 to $70,000. Most e-commerce owners we talk to have never priced long-term care, and the number always catches them off guard.

For multi-channel sellers, this isn't just a family issue. Your net worth is likely tied up in inventory and a business account, not a steady paycheck with employer long-term care coverage behind it. That means the funding decision, whether it's insurance, self-funding, or the VA Aid and Attendance benefit for veterans, has to be made years ahead, alongside your retirement and estate plan.

Andrew Berg, CPA, walks clients through exactly this tradeoff in his outsourced CFO work: how much stays in the business, how much moves to personal accounts, and how much gets earmarked for care.
