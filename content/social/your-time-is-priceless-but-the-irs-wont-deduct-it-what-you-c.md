## LinkedIn

Volunteer hours are never deductible, even at a $150-an-hour market rate. That surprises almost every board member and volunteer we work with. What is deductible: mileage at 14 cents per mile, parking, supplies you buy for a drive, and donated inventory at cost basis, not retail price. A 15-mile round trip to a food bank every Saturday adds up to roughly $109 in deductible mileage over a year, and that's before parking or supplies. For e-commerce sellers clearing slow-moving SKUs before year-end, the cost-basis donation deduction is one of the most overlooked moves we see, especially when inventory accounting isn't accrual-based across Amazon, Shopify, and Walmart. Once a single contribution hits $250, the IRS requires a written acknowledgment from the organization dated before you file. ## Facebook

You can't deduct the value of your volunteer hours, not even the graphic designer who donates 20 hours of $150/hour work to a nonprofit. But mileage (14 cents a mile), supplies, and donated inventory at cost basis often are deductible. A weekly 15-mile round trip to volunteer adds up to about $109 a year in mileage alone.
