## LinkedIn

Most multi-channel sellers we talk with stop counting recurring software charges somewhere around 15, then admit they don't recognize three or four of the vendor names. That's subscription creep, and for businesses running Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart storefronts at once, it compounds fast: twelve tools averaging $65 a month adds up to $9,360 a year. At an 8 percent net margin, that's roughly $117,000 in extra sales just to break even on spend nobody's using.

Fixing it doesn't take a special project. Flag new recurring vendors the moment they hit the bank feed, then run a quarterly review where every tool gets a named owner and a real reason to keep running. Andrew C. Berg, CPA, breaks down why clean categorization matters even more once a business moves from cash-basis to accrual reporting for bank or investor purposes.

Read the full breakdown, including the quarterly checklist we use with clients: https://www.bergpartners.com/resources/take-back-control-of-subscription-creep
