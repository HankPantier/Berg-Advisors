## LinkedIn

A Shopify and Amazon seller running $80,000 a month in inventory, freight, and ad spend can earn $12,000 to $18,000 a year in credit card rewards without trying. Most owners treat that as a bonus. The IRS treats it as a rebate: a $750 cash-back reward on $50,000 in inventory spend reduces cost of goods sold by 1.5 percent, not taxable revenue. Sign-up bonuses without a spending requirement are a different story and can be taxed.

For multi-channel sellers, the real cost shows up in the bookkeeping. Coding rewards as miscellaneous income overstates revenue and can trigger gross receipts tax exposure in some states. Coded correctly, as a contra-expense against the spend category, your P&L stays accurate and your margin reflects reality.
