## LinkedIn

A $50,000 payout on $300,000 in torched inventory. That's what happens when sellers assume their 3PL's fine print covers a warehouse fire, and it usually caps reimbursement well below replacement cost.

Multi-channel sellers buy insurance the way they buy inventory: react to a problem, patch it, move on. A supplier wants proof of product liability, a 3PL wants a certificate, and two years later you've got four overlapping policies while your actual inventory value or personal net worth sits unchecked.

The fix is three numbers: personal net worth for umbrella limits, peak inventory value for cargo coverage, and Amazon's $1 million product liability minimum for the strictest marketplace requirement you face. Review annually, not reactively.

Andrew C. Berg, CPA, breaks down how to size coverage to your real exposure instead of a vendor checklist: https://www.bergpartners.com/resources/insuring-where-it-counts-avoid-over-insuring-in-the-wrong-pl
