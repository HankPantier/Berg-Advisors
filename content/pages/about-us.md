## A CPA firm that treats your business like family

Founded more than 30 years ago in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania, Berg Advisors started as a local CPA firm and grew into a virtual practice serving e-commerce sellers, family offices, and property management companies nationwide. We kept the part that mattered: knowing our clients by name, not by ticket number.

Most local accounting firms treat you like a transaction, an annual filing, a once-a-year phone call in March. We built something different. Every client gets a written mutual success plan outlining what we're tracking together and how often we'll talk about it. That's the difference between a firm that files your return and one that actually knows your business.

If you searched for a CPA in Chester County and found a firm that still answers the phone like a hometown practice, you found the right one. We just happen to serve clients from Newtown Square to Nevada.

## Over 30 years of experience behind every recommendation

Andrew Berg, CPA, founded the firm on the belief that good advice comes from pattern recognition, seeing what works and what doesn't across hundreds of businesses over three decades. That experience shows up in the details: the sales tax nexus question you didn't know to ask, the accrual conversion your bank will require before your next loan, the entity structure that saves real money at tax time.

The firm's approach has earned outside recognition too. Berg Advisors was named a Woodard Top 50 Client Accounting Services firm in 2023 and was a finalist for Canopy's Innovation Award in 2021. Andrew has spoken to AICPA audiences and discussed the firm's approach to modern accounting on the Profit First Professionals podcast.

A CPA credential means someone passed an exam. Three decades of client relationships means someone knows what actually happens when a Shopify seller expands into a fourth state, or when a family office needs to untangle personal and business accounts. That's the kind of experience behind every recommendation we make.

<!-- block: industry-cards | variant: 3-col | theme: ink -->
## Deep specialization in the industries we know best

Generalist CPAs can handle a basic tax return for almost any business. But multi-channel e-commerce, family offices, and property management each carry accounting problems a once-a-year preparer will miss, inventory accrual, multi-state sales tax nexus, consolidated reporting across a dozen properties or entities. We built our practice around three niches instead of trying to be everything to everyone.

### Multi-Channel E-commerce
icon: Globe

Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart each report revenue differently, and reconciling them by hand creates a mess by Q2. We handle [accrual-basis accounting, inventory costing, and multi-state sales tax nexus](/industries/e-commerce) for product-based sellers, as one of the few full-service U.S. firms built specifically for this.

### Family Offices
icon: Briefcase

Multiple entities, multiple accounts, and investments that don't fit a single ledger. We consolidate the view and keep [personal and business finances properly separated](/industries/family-offices), so nothing gets commingled and nothing gets missed at tax time.

### Property Management Companies
icon: Building2

Tracking income, expenses, and owner distributions across a dozen properties in spreadsheets doesn't scale. We give you [one centralized view of performance by property and by entity](/industries/property-management), built for portfolios that keep growing.

## How we work with you

Most accounting relationships run on two things: hourly invoices and reactive service. Ours doesn't.

- [Flat monthly fees](/pricing-calculator), not surprise invoices. You know exactly what you're paying before the month starts, whether we're deep in a sales tax nexus study or just running payroll.
- Proactive communication. We don't wait for you to call with a problem. Quarterly strategy calls are built into every engagement, and if something in your numbers looks off, we're the ones picking up the phone.
- A mutual success plan. At the start of every engagement, we write down what we're tracking, how often we'll meet, and what working well looks like for your business specifically, not a generic checklist.

This is what year-round tax planning and outsourced accounting are supposed to look like: predictable cost, regular contact, and a plan you actually helped write.

## A team invested in your long-term success

Behind every account at Berg Advisors is a small team of CPAs and EAs, not a rotating cast of junior staff you meet once a year. Joshua Beaugrand, CPA, and Lindsey Coburn, CPA, handle much of the day-to-day tax and accounting work. Veil Velarde, CPA, leads outsourced accounting engagements for growth-stage businesses that need fractional back-office support. Julia Lindner, EA, represents clients directly to the IRS when a notice or audit shows up in the mailbox.

We explain things in plain English, not accountant-speak, because the goal is for you to understand your own numbers, not just receive them. Michael Laudazio handles the multi-entity, multi-state compliance work that keeps growing businesses out of trouble with state tax authorities. It's a small team on purpose: fewer handoffs, fewer people who don't know your name. You can [meet the full team here](/about-us/meet-the-team).

Berg Advisors started in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania, but the practice runs virtually today, with clients in more than 20 states, from e-commerce sellers in California to property management companies in Florida and family offices in New York. Cloud-based tools mean your bookkeeper sees the same numbers you do, in real time, whether you're down the road in Chester County or three time zones away.

If you're searching for a CPA near Newtown Square, we're still here, and we still take questions seriously in a first conversation. If you found us from Seattle or Austin instead, the service is the same: the same fixed monthly fee, the same quarterly calls, the same team who knows your business by name.
