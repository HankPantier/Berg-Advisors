## A CPA firm that treats your business like family

Founded more than 30 years ago in Pennsylvania, Berg Advisors is a virtual firm serving e-commerce sellers, family offices, and property management companies nationwide. We prioritize building relationships with our clients and getting to know their stories, so we can provide the services they need most.

Most local accounting firms treat you like a transaction, an annual filing or a once-a-year phone call in March, but we built something different. Every client gets a written mutual success plan outlining what we're tracking together and how often we'll talk about it. That's the difference between a firm that files your return and one that actually knows your business.

<!-- block: content-split | variant: image-right | image: andrew-berg-cpa-review.jpg | alt: "CPA reviewing financial statements with client documents on desk" | query: "cpa reviewing financial documents office" -->
## Over 30 years of experience behind every recommendation

Berg Advisors was founded on the belief that good advice comes from pattern recognition, seeing what works and what doesn't across hundreds of businesses over three decades. That experience shows up in the details: the sales tax nexus question you didn't know to ask, the accrual conversion your bank will require before your next loan, the entity structure that saves real money at tax time.

The firm's approach has earned outside recognition too. Berg Advisors was named a Woodard Top 50 Client Accounting Services firm in 2023 and was a finalist for Canopy's Innovation Award in 2021. Firm leaders have spoken to AICPA audiences and discussed the firm's approach to modern accounting on the Profit First Professionals podcast.

A CPA credential means someone passed an exam. Three decades of client relationships means someone knows what actually happens when a Shopify seller expands into a fourth state, or when a family office needs to untangle personal and business accounts. That's the kind of experience behind every recommendation we make.

<!-- block: industry-cards | variant: 3-col | theme: ink -->
## Deep specialization in the industries we know best

Generalist CPAs can handle a basic tax return for almost any business. But multi-channel e-commerce, family offices, and property management each carry accounting problems a once-a-year preparer will miss, inventory accrual, multi-state sales tax nexus, consolidated reporting across a dozen properties or entities. We built our practice around three niches instead of trying to be everything to everyone.

### Multi-Channel E-commerce
icon: Globe

Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart each report revenue differently, and reconciling them by hand creates a mess by Q2. We handle accrual-basis accounting, inventory costing, and multi-state sales tax nexus for product-based sellers, as one of the few full-service U.S. firms built specifically for this.

### Family Offices
icon: Briefcase

Multiple entities, multiple accounts, and investments that don't fit a single ledger. We consolidate the view and keep personal and business finances properly separated, so nothing gets commingled and nothing gets missed at tax time.

### Property Management Companies
icon: Building2

Tracking income, expenses, and owner distributions across a dozen properties in spreadsheets doesn't scale. We give you one centralized view of performance by property and by entity, built for portfolios that keep growing.
