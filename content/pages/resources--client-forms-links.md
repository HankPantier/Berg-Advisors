## Everything you need to work with us in one place

Starting with a new CPA firm means paperwork. We've tried to make that part painless. This page is the hub for every document, organizer, and checklist you'll need to work with Berg Advisors, whether you're onboarding as a new bookkeeping client, gathering tax documents, or pulling together entity records for a family office review.

Andrew C. Berg, CPA, built the firm's onboarding process around a simple idea: clients shouldn't have to guess what we need from them. With over 30 years of combined CPA experience across the team, we've streamlined intake so you spend less time hunting for forms and more time running your business. If something below doesn't cover your situation, [schedule a consultation](/contact) and we'll sort it out directly.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image | image: client-onboarding-documents.jpg | alt: "Business owner signing an engagement letter with a CPA" | query: "business owner signing document office" -->
## New client onboarding documents

What you'll need depends on which service line you're starting with, bookkeeping, tax, or advisory, but a few documents come up for nearly every new engagement:

- Engagement letter outlining scope of work, timeline, and our fixed monthly fee
- New client intake questionnaire covering entity structure, prior-year filings, and accounting software in use
- IRS Power of Attorney (Form 2848) or Tax Information Authorization (Form 8821) if we'll be representing you before the IRS
- Bank, credit card, and platform access authorization so our outsourced accounting team can begin monthly reconciliations

We'll walk through each one with you on your kickoff call rather than emailing a stack of PDFs and disappearing. That's part of the mutual success plan we build for every client at Berg Advisors, not a one-time form drop. Our [outsourced accounting](/services/accounting) and [tax](/services/tax) teams use slightly different intake packets, so let us know which service you're starting with and we'll send the right set.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: tax-organizer-checklist.jpg | alt: "Tax organizer and folder of financial documents on a desk" | query: "tax documents folder desk" -->
## Tax preparation forms and checklists

Tax prep at Berg Advisors runs year-round, not just January through April, so several of these documents get used more than once a year:

- Individual tax organizer covering W-2, 1099, K-1, and investment income
- Business tax organizer for S-corps, partnerships, and multi-entity filers
- 1099 filing checklist for contractor and vendor payments due each January
- Document upload checklist listing exactly which statements and prior returns to send before your planning call

Because we handle year-round tax planning and strategy, not just annual filing, several clients use these organizers during quarterly check-ins to project liability before year-end rather than scrambling in March. Michael Laudazio works with multi-entity and multi-state filers on compliance timing, and Julia Lindner, EA, handles document requests tied to IRS notices or audit response. Full detail on our approach lives on the [tax services page](/services/tax).

## Resources for e-commerce, property management, and family office clients

Generic checklists don't work for niche businesses, so we built document sets specific to the industries we know best.

### E-commerce Sellers
icon: Globe

Multi-channel sellers on Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart need a sales tax nexus worksheet to track where they've triggered filing obligations, plus a monthly multi-channel sales report template that reconciles revenue across platforms before it hits your books. Our [e-commerce accounting](/industries/e-commerce) team built both from scratch after years of untangling platform-by-platform data by hand.

### Property Management Companies
icon: Building2

Managers juggling multiple properties and entities need owner distribution statements, property-level income and expense templates, and a centralized chart of accounts request form so every property rolls up into one view instead of a dozen spreadsheets. Details on the full engagement live on our [property management](/industries/property-management) page.

### Family Offices
icon: Briefcase

Consolidating visibility across entities and investments starts with an entity and investment summary worksheet, along with a personal-versus-business expense separation checklist to keep records clean for every account under the family umbrella. Our [family office](/industries/family-offices) advisory work is built around exactly this kind of consolidation.

## Secure document upload and client portal access

Berg Advisors is a virtual firm, so the portal isn't a convenience feature, it's how the relationship works. Clients from coast to coast upload bank statements, tax documents, and entity records through an encrypted portal instead of email attachments, and our team reviews and responds through the same system.

The firm was named a Woodard Top 50 Accounting Firm and recognized as an Intuit Firm of the Future, both tied to how we use technology to keep client communication proactive rather than reactive. If you're a new client, your onboarding email includes a portal invitation with setup instructions. Existing clients can log in anytime; if you've misplaced your credentials, reach out and we'll get you back in the same day.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Download Client Forms & Resources | Berg Advisors

**Q: What documents do I need to become a new client at Berg Advisors?**
A: New clients typically need a signed engagement letter, a completed intake questionnaire, and authorization forms for bank or platform access. The exact packet depends on whether you're starting bookkeeping, tax, or advisory services, and your onboarding call will walk through each one.

**Q: Do you offer a secure portal for uploading tax and financial documents?**
A: Yes. Berg Advisors uses an encrypted client portal for uploading bank statements, tax documents, and entity records instead of email attachments. It's built for our nationwide virtual client base and works the same whether you're in Pennsylvania or anywhere else.

**Q: What tax documents do e-commerce sellers need to provide?**
A: Multi-channel sellers should provide monthly sales reports from each platform (Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, Walmart) along with a completed sales tax nexus worksheet, which helps us track where you've triggered multi-state filing obligations.

**Q: How do property management clients submit owner distribution records?**
A: Property management clients use an owner distribution statement template and property-level income and expense reports, which roll into one centralized view across every entity instead of separate spreadsheets.

**Q: What if I need a form that isn't listed on this page?**
A: Contact your Berg Advisors CPA directly. We build one-off paperwork for lender requests, unusual entity structures, or state-specific filings rather than expecting every client to fit a standard checklist.

Not every client fits neatly into a checklist, and we'd rather have a conversation than send you searching through a resource library. If you need a form specific to your entity structure, your state, or a one-off situation like a lender request, contact your Berg Advisors CPA directly.

That's the difference between a document library and a firm that treats you like family: someone answers. [Schedule a consultation](/contact) and we'll get you the exact paperwork you need, or point you to the right person on the team.
