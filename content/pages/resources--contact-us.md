## We're ready to help your business grow

"We Can Help" isn't a slogan we picked because it sounded nice. It's the answer we give when a multi-channel [e-commerce](/industries/e-commerce) seller can't reconcile Amazon and Shopify revenue, when a [property management](/industries/property-management) company can't get a straight answer on owner distributions, or when a [family office](/industries/family-offices) needs one clear view across a dozen entities. Berg Advisors is a virtual CPA firm headquartered in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania, serving clients across the country. Whether you're down the street or three time zones away, the process is the same: real conversations, plain-English answers, and a plan built around your business. If you're ready to stop guessing and start planning, we're ready to talk.

<!-- block: content-prose -->
## Schedule your free consultation

A consultation with Berg Advisors starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch. You'll talk with a CPA or advisor who wants to understand your business first: how you're structured, what's keeping you up at night, and where you want to be in twelve months. We'll ask about your entity type, your industry, and the systems you're already using, whether that's QuickBooks, A2X, AppFolio, or something else entirely. From there, we walk through what working together could look like, including the mutual success plan every client gets, spelling out goals and responsibilities on both sides. No hourly clock runs during this call, and nobody's asking you to sign anything on the spot. Explore our [services](/services) beforehand if you want a head start on the conversation.

## Ways to reach our team

Call us, email us, or fill out the short form on this page. However you reach out, someone on our team picks it up and routes it to the right advisor. Because Berg Advisors bills flat monthly fees instead of the clock, you never have to think twice before sending a quick question or asking us to jump on a call. Proactive communication is built into how we work with every client, not something you have to chase down.

## Visit us in Newtown Square or connect virtually

Berg Advisors is headquartered in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania, where the firm got its start more than 30 years ago. Local clients are always welcome to sit down across the table. But the firm runs as a virtual practice for most of its client base, using secure portals and video calls to serve e-commerce sellers, property management companies, and family offices coast to coast. Location was never really the point. Having a CPA who picks up the phone, knows your books, and treats your business like it matters, that's the point, whether you're a mile from our office or a few states away. Read more about the [firm's background](/about-us) and the team behind it.

## Tell us about your business before we talk

A little context before your consultation goes a long way. The short form below asks about your industry, whether you're structured as an LLC, S-corp, or something else, and the biggest challenge you're trying to solve, whether that's reconciling Amazon and Shopify revenue, tracking owner distributions across a dozen properties, or getting one consolidated view of family entities. The more detail you give us, the more useful that first conversation will be. If you're not sure how to categorize your business, just describe it in your own words. We'll sort out the rest once we're on the phone, whether your needs fall under [accounting](/services/accounting), [tax](/services/tax), or [advisory](/services/advisory) work.
