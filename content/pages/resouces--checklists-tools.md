## Practical tools built from real client work

At Berg Advisors, these checklists are not marketing giveaways. They are the same working documents Andrew C. Berg, CPA, and the team use with e-commerce sellers, property management companies, and family offices across the country every month. Thirty years of CPA work taught us that a good checklist catches a bad quarter before it happens, not just a messy folder after the fact.

Each tool below started as an internal process, something built to keep a client's books accurate between calls. We cleaned them up and made them downloadable because the same gaps show up repeatedly: unreconciled marketplace deposits, missing 1099s, owner distributions nobody tracked. Pick the ones that match your business in our [industries](/industries) work and use them as a starting point for a conversation, not a substitute for one.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-right | image: ecommerce-checklist.jpg | alt: "Business owner reviewing multi-channel sales reports on a laptop" | query: "ecommerce seller reviewing sales reports" -->
## Checklists for e-commerce sellers

Multi-channel selling creates a reconciliation problem no spreadsheet fixes by itself. Revenue lands in Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart accounts on different schedules, with different fee structures and different definitions of a completed sale. Our [e-commerce](/industries/e-commerce) checklists walk through the line items most sellers miss.

- Monthly marketplace reconciliation checklist covering Amazon Seller Central, Shopify, and Walmart Marketplace deposit timing
- Inventory accounting checklist for COGS, landed cost, and accrual-basis adjustments
- Multi-state sales tax nexus tracker to flag when a state's economic threshold has been crossed
- Platform fee and chargeback review list for Amazon FBA and Shopify Payments

These come out of work Andrew Berg, CPA, has done building accrual-basis books for growing e-commerce brands, one of the areas where Berg Advisors operates as a full-service U.S. firm.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: property-management-checklist.jpg | alt: "Property manager reviewing owner distribution statements at a desk" | query: "property manager reviewing financial documents" -->
## Checklists for property management companies

Running books for one property is straightforward. Running them for a dozen properties, three entities, and a handful of owners expecting monthly statements is a different job. Our [property management](/industries/property-management) checklists are organized the way the work actually happens: by entity, then by property, then by owner.

- Monthly owner distribution checklist confirming reserve balances before funds go out
- Multi-property expense categorization guide for shared costs like landscaping, insurance, and management fees
- Entity-by-entity month-end close checklist to keep each LLC's books separate and audit-ready
- Trust account reconciliation checklist for security deposits and prepaid rent

These grew out of ongoing bookkeeping work for property management clients, where one centralized view across every property replaces the spreadsheet juggling most firms leave owners to sort out on their own.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-right | image: family-office-checklist.jpg | alt: "Family office advisor reviewing consolidated investment statements" | query: "financial advisor reviewing investment documents" -->
## Checklists for family offices

Family offices carry a specific kind of complexity: multiple entities, multiple bank accounts, and a real risk of personal and business transactions blending together over time. Our [family office](/industries/family-offices) checklists focus on the two problems that come up most often in these engagements.

- Multi-entity consolidation checklist for combining investment, real estate, and operating entity reporting into one view
- Personal versus business transaction separation guide, built for households managing investment and operating expenses through shared accounts
- Annual entity structure review checklist to confirm each holding is still reporting the way it should

Family offices work with Berg Advisors to get consolidated visibility without hiring a full internal finance team, and these tools reflect what that consolidation actually requires.

<!-- block: checklist-section | variant: with-image-left | image: tax-close-checklist.jpg | alt: "Accountant organizing tax documents and folders on a desk" | query: "accountant organizing tax documents" -->
## Year-round tax and close checklists for every client

Some checklists apply no matter what industry a business is in. These are the ones sent to nearly every [tax and accounting](/services/tax) client at some point in the year, regardless of whether they sell on Amazon, manage apartment buildings, or run a family office.

- Year-end 1099 preparation checklist covering vendor W-9 collection and filing deadlines
- Monthly close checklist for reconciling bank, credit card, and loan accounts before books are marked final
- Tax document organizer for individuals and businesses preparing for filing season
- New business entity setup checklist for choosing a structure and registering with the right states

Use them on your own, or bring them to a call with a Berg Advisors CPA and work through the gaps together.

## Frequently Asked Questions About Ultimate Consulting Checklists & Tools | Berg Partners

**Q: Are these accounting checklists free to download?**
A: Yes. Every checklist on this page is free, no email gate or sales call required first. They come from internal processes Berg Advisors already uses with e-commerce, property management, and family office clients, and are meant to be a starting point for your own bookkeeping cleanup.

**Q: Do I need to be a Berg Advisors client to use these checklists?**
A: No. The checklists work for any business handling its own books. That said, most people bring them to a consultation once they spot gaps, since a checklist tells you what's missing but not always how to fix it correctly for tax purposes.

**Q: What does the e-commerce accounting checklist cover?**
A: It walks through monthly marketplace reconciliation across Amazon, Shopify, and Walmart, inventory and COGS accounting under accrual basis, and a multi-state sales tax nexus tracker so sellers know when a new state registration is required.

**Q: How often should property management companies close their books?**
A: Monthly, organized by entity and then by property. Berg Advisors' month-end close checklist confirms owner distributions and reserve balances are accurate before statements go out, which prevents disputes with property owners later in the year.
