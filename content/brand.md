# About Berg Advisors

## Identity
Berg Advisors is a CPA firm founded more than 30 years ago, originally based in Newtown Square, Pennsylvania (18 Campus Blvd, Suite 100, Room 103), and now operating as a fully virtual firm serving clients across the country and around the world with no geographic concentration. The firm is led by Managing Partner Andrew C. Berg, CPA, who has practiced as a CPA for over 30 years and built the firm around being a strategic advisor and proactive partner rather than a once-a-year service provider. Berg Advisors is a member of AICPA, PASBA, and PICPA, and is affiliated with Woodard, Canopy, and Profit First Professionals. The team includes Partners Megan M. Murray and Joshua Beaugrand, CPA; Director of Client Services Lindsey Coburn, CPA; Director of Outsourced Accounting Veil Velarde, CPA; Director of Compliance/Tax Michael Laudazio; Senior Accountant Julia Lindner, EA; and Senior Accountant Karl Louie Hipolito. Berg Advisors has been recognized as a Woodard Top 50 Client Accounting Services Award Firm (2023 Spotlight), an Intuit Firm of the Future, and a Canopy 2021 Innovation Award Finalist. Andrew Berg has spoken at AICPA conferences and appeared as a guest on the Profit First Professionals podcast.

## Positioning & Differentiation
Berg Advisors positions itself as the CPA firm that treats clients' businesses like family — combining proactive communication, flat monthly fees, and more than 30 years of expertise across e-commerce, property management, and family offices. The firm's brand voice is relationship-driven, proactive, approachable, reassuring, technology-forward, and expert, favoring plain-English explanations over jargon.

Clients typically come to Berg Advisors because they need a firm they can actually talk to and ask questions of, because their small business has outgrown a previous accounting firm, or because they are seeking next-level advice for business strategy rather than year-end compliance alone. Key differentiators include:

- Over 30 years of CPA experience under Managing Partner Andrew C. Berg
- One of the few full-service U.S. firms specializing in e-commerce accrual accounting, inventory accounting, and multi-state sales tax economic nexus
- Fixed, all-inclusive flat monthly fees instead of hourly billing
- Relationship-driven, proactive communication with a mutual success plan for every client
- Woodard Top 50 Accounting Firm and Intuit Firm of the Future recognition

Competitors in the e-commerce accounting space — including SellerCPA, Acuity, Acenteus CCA, AMZ Accountant, and Ledger Labs — focus primarily on bookkeeping and tax for Amazon and Shopify sellers. Berg Advisors differentiates by pairing that same e-commerce specialization (FBA inventory, A2X, marketplace facilitator rules, accrual conversion) with broader advisory work across property management and family office clients, and by emphasizing an ongoing advisory relationship over transactional service.

## Industries Served

### Multi-channel E-commerce
Berg Advisors serves Amazon, Shopify, eBay, Etsy, and Walmart sellers who need accounting, bookkeeping, inventory, and sales tax compliance support. Value proposition: managing financial data from multiple sales channels to ensure accuracy and provide insights for growth, with specialization in accrual accounting, inventory, and complex sales tax nexus. Pain points addressed include reconciling revenue and payouts across platforms with no unified view; understanding true profitability after fees, refunds, and COGS; multi-state sales tax nexus exposure and falling behind on filings; cash-basis books that don't reflect real inventory value or satisfy lenders/investors; FBA inventory accounting including stranded inventory and reimbursements; year-end tax surprises from a lack of proactive planning; confusion over marketplace facilitator laws; and scaling from a single channel to multiple channels without clean financials.

### Family Offices
Berg Advisors supports family-owned businesses and wealth structures that need tailored accounting and cash flow strategy. Value proposition: access to experienced financial professionals who free up time, improve accuracy in record-keeping, and provide peace of mind — helping clients manage their money, know their numbers, and know when they can retire. Pain points addressed include lack of consolidated visibility across multiple entities, accounts, and investments; commingling of personal and business finances; absence of a clear cash flow strategy across generations or business units; difficulty structuring ownership and distributions tax-efficiently; succession planning gaps; reliance on generalist CPAs unfamiliar with complex family wealth structures; avoidable year-end tax bills; and time-consuming financial administration that pulls owners away from running the business.

### Property Management Companies
Berg Advisors works with owner-operators and principals managing residential or commercial rental portfolios. Value proposition: a centralized view of income, expenses, and performance across every property and entity; accurate, on-time owner distributions with a clear paper trail; security deposit and trust accounting handled in full compliance with state regulations; year-end 1099 preparation for vendors and contractors; cash flow reporting by property; end-to-end multi-state filing management; clean separation of business and personal finances; and back-office systems built to scale with the portfolio. Pain points addressed include tracking income and expenses across multiple properties and entities with no centralized view; managing owner distributions; security deposit and trust accounting compliance; time-consuming and error-prone 1099 filing; limited cash flow visibility across a growing portfolio; multi-state filing complexity; mixed personal and business finances; and scaling the portfolio without matching back-office systems.
